- the actual republic
	- power split between consuls (monarchy), senate (aristocracy), and popular assemblies (democracy) -- Polybius thought this prevented tyranny. Mixed constitution
	- no one holds permanent power: consuls served 1 year terms in pairs (to veto over each other)
	- civic virtue: citizens who subordinate private interest to public good -> the republic depends on them.
	- liberty is non-domination not non-interference
	- class conflict was good -- Plebeian/patrician tension produced the tribunes, who could veto any magistrate's action. Institutionalized opposition
	- died from concentration of military power in individuals, wealth inequality, and the breakdown of norms (limits on terms, fixed career ladder, vetos, approval) with no legal enforcement  *** the republic could not scale to govern an empire. 

a series of events
- ~509: republic founded: two consuls replace monarchy
- 494: plebs walk out of Rome; "secession" --> tribune of plebs made to veto any magistrate
- 451-449: twelve tables written, first written roman law --> matters because things are no longer up for interpretation
- 390: gauls sack rome. drives Roman militarism
- 343-390: samnite wars, rome conquers central italy
- 287: Lex Hortensia, a law that made Plebian assemblies' decisions binding on all citizens. aristocracy still persisted through wealth though
- 265: Punic wars, where Rome becomes major Mediterranean power
- 133-121: Gracchi brothers -- try to redistribute land to poor, get murdered by senators, political violence becomes normalised, republic norms start breaking
- 107: Marius reforms army, loyal to commander because they pay
- slowly senate gets sidelined as army power concentrates in individuals (Caesar, Pompey, Crassus)
- Caesar conquers Gaul, then defeats Pompey
- Caesar gets assassinated on the Ides of March by conspirators who think they're saving the republic
- Second Triumvirate (Octavian, Anthony, Lepidus) -- legally sanctioned dictatorship to 'restore the republic'
- Death of Cicero: targeted by Anthony, among thousands
- Octavian defeats Anthony and Cleopatra in Battle of Actium
- Octavian becomes Augustus. Republic is over -- he holds all the power.
